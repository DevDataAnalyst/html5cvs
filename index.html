GIS Mapping of My Local Area

Study Area: Chandaka, Similipatana, Bhubaneswar, Odisha

Introduction

Geographic Information System (GIS) is a technology used to collect, store, analyze and display information related to places on a map.

Our Local Area

This project shows a simple GIS map of the local area around Chandaka, Similipatana, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. The map represents important public features such as roads, schools, health facilities, markets and green areas.

Click a marker or road for details. Use the checkboxes to show or hide each layer.

Important Features

  • Main Roads
  • School
  • Health Centre/Hospital
  • Market
  • Green Area/Park
  • Important Public Places

Uses of GIS

  1. Helps us understand locations easily.
  2. Helps in planning roads and public facilities.
  3. Helps identify important places on a map.
  4. Helps in managing natural and urban areas.
  5. Makes geographical information easier to understand.

Conclusion

GIS is a useful technology for studying and managing our local area. It helps us understand different places and their locations through maps.

Note: This is an illustrative school-project map and does not show an exact private house location.
